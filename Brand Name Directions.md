# Brand Name Directions

## Vela

**Meaning / perceived meaning:** A vela is a sail; it evokes navigation, direction, and forward movement. It also has a subtle celestial association through the Vela constellation.

**Tagline:** *Money, in your direction.*

**Brand vibe:** The strongest all-around direction. Minimal, assured, and global—like a modern financial institution built for movement rather than a crypto app built for traders. Visually, Vela could live in a refined black, white, and electric-blue system: calm, premium, and precise.

---

## Sable

**Meaning / perceived meaning:** A deep black shade and a traditionally premium material. It signals composure, value, and security without being overly literal about custody or wealth.

**Tagline:** *Keep your money working.*

**Brand vibe:** Dark, restrained, and quietly luxurious. Sable feels more mature than playful—well suited to a highly polished account experience that makes stablecoins feel safe, useful, and everyday. It is closer to a premium card brand than a crypto-native protocol.

---

## Nest

**Meaning / perceived meaning:** A home, a protected place, and a familiar reference to a nest egg. It makes the account feel personal and useful: a place where money can sit, grow, and remain available for everyday life.

**Tagline:** *A place for your money to grow.*

**Brand vibe:** Warm, simple, and broadly consumer-friendly. Nest brings a more human, everyday feeling to on-chain finance—less like a trading product and more like the account people build their financial life around. The identity could be soft and refined, with restrained color, generous space, and an especially simple product experience.

---

## Cove

**Meaning / perceived meaning:** A protected place by the water: calm, secure, and connected to a wider world. It suggests a place where money can rest safely, stay useful, and move when needed.

**Tagline:** *A better place for your money.*

**Brand vibe:** Clean, serene, and quietly premium. Cove has the warmth of Nest with a more global and sophisticated edge. It could support a polished, modern-fintech identity built around deep navy, soft neutrals, subtle movement, and a product experience that makes on-chain money feel effortless rather than technical.

Before committing, each finalist needs formal trademark, domain, app-store, and social-handle clearance in the relevant financial-services classes and launch geographies.
