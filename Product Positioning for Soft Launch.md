# Product Positioning for Soft Launch

## Core Positioning

Tetra should launch as **your crypto neobank**: a self-custodial account for global users.

The category should feel familiar enough for mainstream users to understand, but differentiated enough for crypto-native users to care. Tetra is not positioned as another wallet, exchange, trading terminal, yield farm, or card app. It is positioned as the account layer that brings those behaviors together.

The simple user promise:

**Your crypto neobank.**

Tetra gives users one on-chain financial account where they can hold digital dollars, trade global markets, earn on eligible idle balances, and spend from the same account.

The key positioning idea is that Tetra turns stablecoins from a passive balance into an active financial account. Today, stablecoins are mostly used as money in-between actions: held on exchanges, stored in wallets, moved into DeFi protocols, or off-ramped when users need to spend. Tetra should make stablecoins feel like the center of a user's financial life: the place where money can sit productively, move easily, and be deployed into market opportunities.

For launch, the sharpest wedge is still spendable yield:

**Your stablecoins earn until you spend them.**

But the larger narrative should be:

**Tetra is your crypto neobank: the self-custodial account for holding, trading, earning, and spending with digital dollars.**

## The Product Concept

Tetra is not just a yield product, card product, or trading terminal. It is an on-chain account that connects the main things users want to do with money:

1. **Hold** digital dollars in a self-custodial account.
2. **Earn** yield while balances are idle.
3. **Spend** through a card without manually off-ramping.
4. **Trade** crypto, RWAs, stocks, commodities, forex, and other global markets.

The core idea is:

**Your money should stay useful at every stage: while it sits, while you spend, and when you want to trade.**

## Why This Works For Soft Launch

The soft launch should introduce the full account concept from the beginning. Visitors should understand that Tetra is their crypto neobank, even while access is still waitlist-only.

The practical account loop makes the proposition concrete:

**Hold digital dollars -> trade when opportunity appears -> earn while capital waits -> spend when needed.**

This keeps the page broad enough to establish the category, but focused enough to explain why the account matters. The correct framing is:

**Tetra is your crypto neobank. Trade, Earn, and Spend are the ways your money works inside it.**

## Product Pillars

### 1. Account

The Account pillar is the foundation of the product. It is the crypto-neobank layer: one self-custodial place to sign in, hold digital dollars, see balances, manage yield, spend, and access trading. Trade, Earn, and Spend should be presented as capabilities of this account rather than separate products.

The key product idea is abstraction. Users should not have to think about wallets, chains, gas, seed phrases, bridges, or individual protocols before getting value. The account layer is what turns Earn, Spend, and Trade from separate features into one financial relationship.

### 2. Trade

Trade is a core capability of the Tetra account. Over time, the trading layer should let users access crypto, RWAs, stocks, commodities, forex, and other global markets from the same account.

Trade should be positioned as global market access from the same financial account, not as a separate terminal. It gives users a reason to keep opportunity capital in Tetra; Earn and Spend make that account useful when capital is not in a position.

### 3. Earn

Earn is the initial deposit hook. It gives users a reason to move funds into Tetra before every product is fully built out: their stablecoins should not sit idle.

Earn should not be positioned as a complex DeFi workflow. It should feel like a native property of the account: when funds are not being spent or traded, they can work in the background through curated yield opportunities.

### 4. Spend

Spend is what makes the account useful in the real world. It solves the manual off-ramp problem where users need to sell crypto, withdraw to a bank, wait, and then spend.

The key concept is continuity. The same balance that earns should also be available when users need to use it. This is what makes "spendable yield" strong: money works until the moment it is spent.

## Product Loop

The products should feel connected, not like separate verticals.

The intended loop:

1. User deposits stablecoins.
2. Idle funds earn yield.
3. User can spend from the same balance.
4. User can move capital into trades when there is an opportunity.
5. Trading profits can return to the account and continue earning.

This is the larger Tetra concept:

**Your crypto neobank: one self-custodial account where money can hold, trade, earn, and spend without leaving the system.**

## Long-Term Narrative

The soft launch should not position Tetra as "everything finance" on day one. That is too broad.

Instead, Tetra should use a staged narrative:

1. **Now:** spendable yield account.
2. **Next:** stablecoin neobank.
3. **Long-term:** complete on-chain finance account for earning, spending, and global trading.

This gives the launch a simple wedge while keeping the full product vision intact.

## Language Guidelines

Use:

- Stablecoin neobank
- Self-custodial account
- Digital dollar account
- Earn on idle balances
- Spend from the same balance
- Trade global markets
- On-chain finance account
- Variable yield

Avoid:

- Bank account
- Guaranteed yield
- Risk-free yield
- FDIC-insured
- Deposit account
- Savings account
