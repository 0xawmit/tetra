# Tetra Workspace

## Current Context

Tetra will soft launch next week. The soft launch is an announcement and waitlist push only; the product will not be live yet.

The product beta is expected to go live in about one month. At beta launch, the product should include the major pillars: Trade, Earn, and Spend.

The current positioning direction from the founder is **stablecoin neobank**. Tetra should be framed as one self-custodial account where global users can hold digital dollars, earn yield, spend, and trade global markets.

## Product Scope

### Soft Launch

- Public announcement
- Waitlist collection
- Litepaper published publicly
- Website copy aligned around the stablecoin neobank narrative
- X content begins building narrative from soft launch to beta launch

### Beta / Hard Launch

- Product goes live for users
- Users can access Trade, Earn, and Spend
- Trading scope includes RWAs, stocks, ETFs, and crypto
- Earn should be positioned as idle stablecoin yield
- Spend should be positioned around card-based real-world utility

## Core Narrative

Tetra is a self-custodial stablecoin neobank for global users.

The product starts with a simple idea:

**One account to earn, spend, and trade globally.**

The soft launch should not over-explain every feature, but it should clearly establish the full product concept:

- Users hold digital dollars in one account.
- Idle balances can earn yield.
- Funds can be spent through a card.
- Capital can be used to trade RWAs, stocks, ETFs, and crypto.

## My Role

My initial responsibility is product positioning, narrative, and go-to-market foundation.

The priority is to help the team communicate Tetra clearly before soft launch, then build enough public narrative and educational material to carry the company into beta launch.
